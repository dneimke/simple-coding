Mode 1 · Sideline & Tech Bench

1. Live Match (Real-Time Pitch Capture)

Start Live Match

Designed for coaches, team managers, or analysts watching live play. Tap Start when the whistle blows and log events using large, high-contrast team-coloured buttons that never shift position mid-game.

How it speeds up your workflow:
  • Sticky Pitch Zones (1–4): Tap a zone once as the ball moves into that quarter of the pitch; every event button automatically attaches that zone (e.g., FIRE BS2).
  • 0.5-Second Glance Verification: The top bar always shows Last: <Event> · mm:ss with a 1-tap Undo (or Ctrl+Z) for instant recovery from mis-taps.
  • Halftime & Full-Time Ready: Check live team comparisons under Log → Statistics at halftime, then tap Finish → Complete to save and immediately reset for the next match.
Mode 2 · Post-Match Video Tagging

2. From Video (High-Speed Video Coding)

Code From Video

When you couldn't code live—or want every clip locked to the exact video frame—choose From Video. Load your match MP4 and the screen switches into Focus Tagging Mode.

How it speeds up your workflow:
  • Synced to Video Time: There is no separate stopwatch to manage. If you pause, rewind, or scrub the video to 14:20 and tap a button, the event is logged at 14:20 with its pre-roll lead and post-roll lag window.
  • 1.5x / 2x Playback + Skip Controls: Breeze through general midfield play at 2x speed, use +5s / +10s to jump over ball-out-of-bounds delays, and use -5s to replay a circle entry before tagging.
  • Quick Tags & Full Board: Use the 1-tap Quick Tag bar directly under the video, or expand the full custom button board below it. Every tag auto-saves immediately.
Mode 3 · Sportscode & MyVA Integration

3. Import XML (Instant Match Package)

Go to Matches Hub

Already have a Sportscode XML file from a sideline helper, a league video feed, or another device? Choose Import XML to preview the events and create a saved match in seconds.

How it speeds up your workflow:
  • Preserves Lead/Lag Clip Windows: Every imported instance keeps its exact start and end clip timings so clicking an event seeks directly to the build-up of the play (time - lead).
  • 15-Minute Coaching Review: Filter by event type (e.g., PC, Circle Entry, Shot), star (★) your top 6–10 teaching moments, and step through them in full-screen Presentation Mode.
Multi-Pass Workflow

How the Modes Work Together: "Continue Coding"

Try Sample in Tag Mode

You never have to capture every detail in one stressful pass. Regardless of whether a match was created via Live Match, From Video, or Import XML, you can open its ⋯ menu on the Matches screen and select Continue coding.

Pass 1: Fast Core Capture

Have a bench helper capture core events live (Circle Entry, PC, Shot, Goal), or import a base XML package.

Pass 2: Targeted Video Layer

Click Continue coding, load the match video at 2x speed, and layer in specific tactical codes (e.g., Press, Outlet, or Turnover) into the exact same timeline.

Workflow 2 · Button Customizer

Button Board Configuration

Tailor buttons, pitch zones, and clip lead/lag windows to your squad's tactical focus.

Open Board Customizer

Every team plays with different tactical priorities. While casual volunteers need a simple 6-button board, senior analysts need spatial pitch zones, lead/lag clip windows, and dedicated coach codes. Simple-Coding lets you customize, duplicate, and share button boards without modifying code.

Built-in Presets vs. Custom Boards

The app includes protected factory templates like Simple (6 Events) (self-explanatory essentials for drafted helpers) and Hockey One Zoned (advanced analyst shorthand with pitch zones 1–4).

Click Duplicate on any preset to create a fully customizable team board (e.g., Under-16 Pressing or Senior Penalty Corners).

Pre-Roll Lead & Post-Roll Lag Windows

When exporting to Sportscode XML or reviewing video, each button has a custom clip window:

  • Pre-roll Lead (seconds): Clip starts seconds before your tap. For example, SHOT has 15s lead so the clip captures the build-up carry into the circle.
  • Post-roll Lag (seconds): Clip continues seconds after your tap. For example, PC has 10s lag to capture the entire injection and shot outcome.
Sharing Boards Across Your Coaching Staff

Under Configure → Actions, click Export file to save your board file. Send it to assistants or volunteers who can Import file or paste it on their own phone or tablet with 1 tap.

Workflow 3 · Tactical Telemetry

Rapid Label Sweep & AI-Ready Metadata

Transform raw match timestamps into deep causal tactical intelligence in under 60 seconds.

Try Label Sweep in Sample Match

To get true tactical depth out of video analysis or AI coaching prompts, you need discriminators—like which channel a circle entry came from, or how a penalty corner broke down. But forcing 20 new buttons onto a live sideline phone destroys eyes-on-pitch focus.

Simple-Coding solves this with a two-pillar architecture: Zero-look live capture on the sideline, combined with a 60-second rapid-fire video sweep post-match.

1. The Sequential "Label Sweep" (60-Second Video Pass)

In Match Review, click Sweep (N). The video automatically jumps 3 seconds before each unlabelled clip, docked with 1-tap Pill Chips and keyboard hotkeys:

Tapping a chip immediately saves the label and flies to the next unlabelled event. 20 circle entries can be fully categorized by flank (Left, Center, Right) in under one minute.

2. Timeline Badges & "Unlabelled" Filter

Labelled events display prominent semantic badges directly in the timeline list (e.g. [ Left ], [ Direct ]).

Toggle the Unlabelled (N) filter tab to instantly isolate pending events. As you tag them, they automatically clear from the pending queue.

3. Zero-Toil AI Derivation & The Tactical Dividend

The app automatically analyzes event sequences without asking you to tap extra buttons:

  • Possession Origin: If Ball Won (BW) occurred within 10s before an entry, it is automatically derived as a Counter-Attack (TO); otherwise tagged as Settled Build-Up (BU).
  • Sportscode XML <label> Schema: Labels export inside standard <label> tags, preserving clean timeline rows for Sportscode/MyVA matrices.
  • LLM Telemetry: Feeds high-leverage data into AI coaching prompts—e.g., "Fire generated 65% of circle entries down the right flank, but right-side entries converted to shots at only 12%, whereas central entries converted at 50%."
